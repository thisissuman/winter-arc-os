**Superseded October 3, 2026.** These broad-product concepts are design history only. Use the new simple-habit proposals referenced by `docs/DESIGN_REVIEW.md`, once approved.

Created October 3, 2026 with the built-in image generation tool. These are visual proposals with illustrative data, not implemented application screens or verified user analytics. Both images include desktop and mobile layouts and inherit the existing DESIGN.md system.

## Today

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: high fidelity UI/UX concept board for the existing Winter Arc OS personal tracking web application. Generate a crisp professional raster image, wide landscape composition approximately 1800x1200, with a large desktop app screenshot occupying left 75% and a separate flat mobile app viewport occupying right 23%, on a plain charcoal canvas, aligned at their top. No physical devices, perspective, fancy presentation props, photographic assets, shadows, marketing illustrations or watermarks. A tiny caption at bottom says "UI concept · Illustrative data". Design is an existing approved visual system: background #101115, sidebar #15161b, cards #181a20, subtle raised surfaces #1d2027, borders #2b2d36, foreground #ededf2, muted #a5a7b2, restrained violet #a89af3, success #87d5a4, warning #eac079. Geist-like neutral sans serif, clearly readable small UI text, precise aligned tabular numerals, understated thin outline icons, 8px controls and 14px panel radii, quiet separators, ample but efficient spacing. Flat clean product UI, no gradients or glows or glass. Desktop has a narrow left navigation sidebar titled "Winter Arc OS" with small square violet brand icon, sections "Daily work" with Today, Habits, Metrics, Fitness, Career, Tasks, Goals; "Review" with Insights, Reflection; "Manage" with Challenges, Settings. Relevant route highlighted violet. Mobile has app header and fixed 5-link bottom navigation Today, Track, Plan, Insights, More, with reachable 44px touch controls. Maintain real product distinctions: binary habits, raw numerical measurements, and weekly quotas are different. Match visual identity between both views. Render all requested literal labels accurately.
Primary request: a refined actionable TODAY screen, compact header "Today" with subtitle "Saturday, 3 October 2026", challenge dropdown "Winter Arc 2026" and date arrows. A quiet horizontal challenge strip "Winter Arc 2026" and "33 of 92 days elapsed", slender violet elapsed time bar and caption "Calendar progress". Compact separate Daily score "50%" and Weekly score "62%" cards, both labeled "In progress", never conflate calendar progress with scores. Main hero is immediate logging, not giant decorative scores.
Desktop beneath scores has a dominant "Scheduled habits" region with "View calendar" text action. Morning rows "Creatine" small metadata "5 g · Daily" with green check and "Completed"; "Read" metadata "20 minutes · Daily" with clear outlined "Mark done" button. Evening rows "Stretch" metadata "10 minutes · Daily" with "Mark done"; "Prepare tomorrow" with green check and "Completed". Each row has one title and clear affordance, not card inside card. Beside or beneath habits, "Measurements" has Water "1.5 L" and target "of 3 L" with quiet progress and buttons "+250 ml" "+500 ml"; Protein "Not logged" with input placeholder "grams" and "Save"; Weight "Not logged" small label "Observation" and input placeholder "kg" and "Save". Blank inputs stay blank, no invented readings. Lower section "Weekly and monthly progress": "Workouts" with "2 / 4 sessions this week" and "Log workout" action. Collapsed detail disclosure "How scores are calculated". No plots on Today.
Mobile Today uses same header and context with two small score summaries side by side, habits visible in first viewport; compact rows with same four habits and correct statuses, then Water quick buttons and protein input. Five-link bottom dock Today selected. Mobile view shows the top portion naturally, with more content below viewport. The primary visual impression is: clear next action, fast logging, calm application rhythm.

Primary request: refined HABITS calendar screen, desktop title "Habits" subtitle "Your routines, one shared log." primary violet "+ New habit" button. Toolbar month switcher "October 2026" with left/right arrows, labeled dropdown "Tracking context" showing "Winter Arc 2026", labeled dropdown "Show" showing "Current" and quiet "Apply" control.
Dominant monthly matrix inside one clean panel, with a sticky left identity column and dates as slim columns. Show visible October columns 1 Thu, 2 Fri, 3 Sat, 4 Sun through 14 Wed, horizontal scrollbar indicating rest of 31-day month exists. Highlight October 3 header and subtle vertical today guide in violet; label "Today" near it. Six rows with clear names: "Creatine", "Read", "Stretch", "Prepare tomorrow", "Meditate", "Mobility". Rows are 52px tall with spacious clickable cells. First two dates only can have historical completed green check, missed muted coral x, skipped amber arrow, or unscheduled gray dash. October 3 cells: Creatine green check, Read violet open pending circle, Stretch violet open pending circle, Prepare tomorrow green check, Meditate violet open pending circle, Mobility unscheduled dash. All dates after October 3 are visibly muted future right-arrow marks with no completed or missed cells. Do not fabricate future results. Show a succinct visible legend below: "✓ Completed", "× Missed", "↷ Skipped", "◌ Pending today", "— Not scheduled", "→ Future". Avoid random heatmap blocks or a generic contribution chart.
Below the matrix, compact "Schedules and consistency" list with "Creatine" detail "Daily · Morning" and "2 / 2 closed opportunities" and small "Edit" / "Archive" actions; "Read" detail "Daily · Anytime" and "1 / 2 closed opportunities", "Edit" / "Archive". One small footnote "Today's pending habits are excluded from closed consistency." No giant fake streak badges, score cards, aggregate vanity charts, points, flames, or trophies.
Mobile shows "Habits" header and "+ New habit", October 2026 month navigation, Current filter, and the same matrix with fixed habit identity and only October 1–5 visible; horizontal scrollbar and small instruction "Swipe to review the month", clear large cell touch targets. Same names and statuses as desktop. Visible compact legend and start of schedule list. Mobile bottom dock Track selected. The main visual impression is: readable habit identity, distinct date states, easy editing, truthful monthly history.
